## What We Write About

- **AI & Machine Learning** — Agentic AI, GenAI design patterns, lightweight LLMs, model context protocols
- **System & Software Design** — Architectural principles, scalable system patterns, interview-ready strategies
- **Software Engineering Fundamentals** — Design patterns, data structures & algorithms, solid engineering foundations
- **DevOps & Infrastructure** — Docker, Kubernetes, YAML practices, deployment workflows
- **Programming Languages** — Python and other practical language guides
- **Career & Professional Growth** — Technical interview prep, behavioral interview advice

## Our Philosophy

We believe that understanding _why_ something is built a certain way is just as important as knowing _how_. This blog is our way of documenting deep dives into the concepts, patterns, and principles that shape good software — from theory to practice.
