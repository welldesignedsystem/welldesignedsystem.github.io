## LLM Evaluation
- RAGAS Pytest Framework: A comprehensive testing framework for evaluating LLMs, including unit tests, integration tests, and end-to-end tests. It provides a structured approach to assess the performance and reliability of LLMs in various scenarios.
- 7 Key Evaluation Metrics commonly used to test LLMs:
  - Perplexity: Measures how well a language model predicts a sample. Lower perplexity indicates better performance.
  - BLEU Score: Evaluates the quality of text generated by comparing it to reference texts. Higher BLEU scores indicate better quality.
  - ROUGE Score: Measures the overlap between generated text and reference text, commonly used for summarization tasks.
  - F1 Score: Combines precision and recall to evaluate the accuracy of generated text, especially in classification tasks.
  - Human Evaluation: Involves human judges assessing the quality of generated text based on criteria such as coherence, relevance, and fluency.
  - Diversity Metrics: Assess the variety in generated text, ensuring that the model does not produce repetitive or generic responses.
  - Task-Specific Metrics: Depending on the application (e.g., question answering, translation), specific metrics may be used to evaluate performance.
